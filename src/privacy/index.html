Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

This site is a demo. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking scripts and no cookies, and it does not store anything in your browser.

The demo form

The payment demo is not connected to a bank or a payment provider. If you press the Continue button, your browser opens the sample result page on this same site with the amount, the bank you picked and two fixed sample values in the page address. Like any address, it can show up in the host's request logs. The site itself does nothing with that data, and no payment happens.

Third parties your browser contacts

Your browser loads every page, script, style, font and icon from this site's own address, sg-vanilla-component.engg.app, and contacts no other host.

  • Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any host, it processes standard request data such as your IP address and user agent to deliver pages and may keep it in its logs.

The Manrope typeface is served from this site, not from a font service. The bank marks in the demo are letters drawn by the page, not images from the banks. There are no maps, embeds or analytics. Links to GitHub take you to GitHub, where its own privacy terms apply.

Questions

There is no contact email. If you have a question about privacy, open an issue on GitHub.
