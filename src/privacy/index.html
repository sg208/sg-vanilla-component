Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

This site is a demo. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking scripts and no cookies, and it does not store anything in your browser.

The demo form

The payment demo is not connected to a bank or a payment provider. If you press the Verder (continue) button, your browser sends the amount and the bank you picked to the sample result page on this same site. Nothing reads or keeps that data, and no payment happens.

Third parties your browser contacts

  • portal.bp.nu serves the bank logos in the payment demo. Your browser requests them directly, so that server sees your IP address and the usual request headers.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any host, it processes standard request data such as your IP address and user agent to deliver pages and may keep it in its logs.

The site loads no web fonts, maps or other external scripts. Links to GitHub take you to GitHub, where its own privacy terms apply.

Questions

There is no contact email. If you have a question about privacy, open an issue on GitHub.
