sgsg-vanilla-component

Accessibility

Last updated 1 November 2026

sg-vanilla-component is a small demo of a payment-option picker. I aim for it to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. It does not meet it everywhere yet; the gaps I know about are listed below.

What is in place

  • Every page sets its language to English and has its own title.
  • Every page has one heading 1, a header, a main landmark and a footer with a labelled site navigation.
  • A "Skip to content" link is the first thing you reach with the Tab key and jumps to the main content.
  • Links, buttons, fields and bank options show a visible focus outline when you reach them with the keyboard.
  • Text meets a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 and controls at least 3:1, in both the light and the dark colour scheme. The site follows your system setting for light or dark.
  • Pages reflow down to 320 pixels wide without scrolling sideways, and interactive targets are at least 24 by 24 pixels.
  • If your system asks for reduced motion, the small transitions on buttons and the bank list are switched off.
  • On the payment demo, the euro and cent fields are labelled "Euros" and "Cents" and are tied to the note that gives the minimum and maximum.
  • When the amount is out of range, the error is announced to screen readers, the fields are marked invalid and the bank picker is disabled until the amount is fixed.
  • The bank picker is a button that opens a list of options. Arrow keys, Home and End move through the banks, Enter or Space picks one, and Escape closes the list and returns focus to the button.
  • Decorative icons are hidden from screen readers.

Known limitations

  • The amount fields clear when they receive focus, so you type the amount again rather than editing it. Leaving a field empty restores the previous value.
  • The demo form leads to a sample result page, which only says that it is a sample.

Report a problem

If something on the site gets in your way, please open an issue on GitHub. Tell me which page you were on and what you were trying to do.
