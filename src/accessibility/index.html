Accessibility

Last updated 1 November 2026

sg-vanilla-component is a small demo of a payment-option picker. I aim for it to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. It does not meet it everywhere yet; the gaps I know about are listed below.

What is in place

  • Every page sets its language to English and has its own title.
  • The accessibility, privacy and terms pages use a single heading 1, a main landmark and a footer, and reflow down to 360 pixels wide.
  • Footer links show a visible focus outline when you reach them with the keyboard.
  • On the payment demo, the bank picker opens with Enter or Space and moves focus to the first bank in the list.
  • The edit-amount button has the accessible name "Edit amount".
  • Decorative icons are hidden from screen readers.

Known limitations

  • The payment demo has no heading 1; its first heading is a heading 2.
  • The two amount fields (euros and cents) have no label, so a screen reader announces them only as number fields.
  • The bank picker is built from plain elements without listbox or combobox roles, so assistive technology does not announce it as a list of options.
  • The demo form posts to a sample result page, which only says that it is a sample.
